The one-line version: your ledger lives only on your own device and in your own iCloud, and we cannot see it. We collect anonymous usage statistics only, and you can turn them off.

What we collect

We cannot receive or see your ledger, contacts or receipt photos. Owely has no server of its own, no account system, no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics. What you record is stored only in the local database on your device and, if your device is signed in to iCloud, in iCloud databases kept by Apple under your identity. The only exception is the anonymous usage statistics described below.

Photos, Calendar, and Face ID

The app requests Photo Library access (to attach receipts), Calendar access (to create due-date reminders), and Face ID (for the app lock) only when you actively use those features. Everything runs on-device; receipt amount recognition uses Apple's on-device text recognition, and images never leave your device.

iCloud sync

Starting with version 1.2, if your device is signed in to iCloud, your ledger syncs between your own devices through Apple's CloudKit private database. That data lives under your iCloud account, counts toward your iCloud storage, and is encrypted and held by Apple; we have no server and cannot read it. You can turn off sync for Owely in iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud; your data stays intact on the device.

Linking with a contact

Starting with version 2.1, you can invite another Owely user to link with one of your contacts. Once linked, entries you record for that contact from then on (type, amount, currency, date, note, event name) and the display name you set in the app are stored in an iCloud shared zone that only the two of you can access. The zone lives under the iCloud account of the person who sent the invite and is kept by Apple; we cannot read it. Entries from before linking, entries for other contacts, and receipt photos are never placed there. The other person can see an anonymous, app-specific account code generated by Apple — not your Apple ID, phone number or email. The invite link contains your display name and the share link, and travels through the messaging app you choose; the invite web page does not send these to any server. Either side can unlink at any time; whatever is already in each person’s ledger stays on their own device.

Notifications

If you turn on link notifications, Apple’s iCloud sends a notification straight to your device when the other person records an entry or joins your invite. Notifications contain no amounts, notes or names. They are delivered by Apple; no server of ours is involved and we never receive your device’s push token. You can turn them off in the app’s Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Anonymous usage statistics

Starting with version 2.1, at most once a day the app writes one anonymous record to Apple CloudKit: the date, app version, language and region, whether Pro is unlocked, rough ranges for your number of contacts and entries, and counts of links and invitations. It never includes names, contacts, amounts, notes, photos or device identifiers. Apple attaches an anonymous, app-specific account code to each record; we cannot tell who you are from it. We use these statistics only to understand how many people use Owely and which features help. They are not used for advertising or tracking and are not shared with third parties. The first time you open version 2.1, the app shows this explanation with a switch; nothing is sent before you tap “Get Started”. You can also turn it off anytime in Settings → Anonymous Usage Statistics.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by the Apple App Store. We never see your payment information.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all of your data. We hold no copies. If you used iCloud sync, you can also delete Owely's iCloud data in iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. For linked contacts: after unlinking, the person who sent the invite can delete the shared data in the same place; the person who joined loses access as soon as they unlink.

연결 알림을 켜면 상대가 내역을 기록하거나 초대에 참여할 때 Apple의 iCloud가 기기로 직접 알림을 보냅니다. 알림에는 금액, 메모, 이름이 포함되지 않습니다. 알림은 Apple이 전송하며 당사 서버는 관여하지 않고 기기의 푸시 토큰도 당사가 받지 않습니다. 앱 설정 또는 iOS ‘설정 → 알림’에서 끌 수 있습니다.

익명 사용 통계

2.1 버전부터 앱은 하루 최대 한 번 Apple CloudKit에 익명 기록 한 건을 씁니다: 날짜, 앱 버전, 언어와 지역, Pro 여부, 연락처 수와 기록 건수의 대략적인 구간, 연결 및 초대 횟수. 이름, 연락처, 금액, 사유, 사진, 기기 식별자는 포함하지 않습니다. Apple이 각 기록에 이 앱 전용 익명 계정 코드를 붙이지만 당사는 이를 통해 사용자가 누구인지 알 수 없습니다. 통계는 이용자 수와 유용한 기능을 파악하는 데만 쓰이며 광고나 추적에 쓰지 않고 제3자에게 제공하지 않습니다. 버전 2.1을 처음 열면 이 안내와 스위치를 먼저 보여 드리며, ‘시작하기’를 누르기 전에는 통계를 전송하지 않습니다. 이후에도 ‘설정 → 익명 사용 통계’에서 언제든 끌 수 있습니다.

구매

Pro는 일회성 구매이며 결제와 환불은 모두 Apple App Store가 처리합니다. 당사는 결제 정보를 볼 수 없습니다.

데이터 삭제

앱을 삭제하면 모든 데이터가 삭제됩니다. 당사는 어떤 사본도 보관하지 않습니다. iCloud 동기화를 사용했다면 iOS 설정 → Apple 계정 → iCloud → 계정 저장 공간 관리에서 Owely의 iCloud 데이터도 삭제할 수 있습니다. 연결 공유 데이터: 연결을 해제한 뒤 초대한 쪽은 같은 위치에서 삭제할 수 있고, 참여한 쪽은 해제하는 즉시 접근할 수 없게 됩니다.
